FAQ

Does the app take student payments online?

Fees are paid to the center directly. The app records payments and issues receipts. We never receive or store card details.

Who can see student data?

Centers and teachers see their own students only, parents see their own children only, and no center sees another center's data.

Does the app track my location?

No. Location is compared to the center area at check-in only and is not stored.

Do you sell our data?

No. We do not sell or rent data and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

Does a child need an account?

A child under 18 is activated only with a parent's consent, which the parent can withdraw at any time.

What happens to my data if the subscription ends?

Your data stays saved and the services return after renewal. If an account is closed, data stays available for export for 30 days.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new center gets a free trial with all features, and parents get the first month free.
