Center, Teacher and Data Processing Agreement

Version: 2026-11-v3

This Agreement applies to every Center or teacher that creates a workspace on the Platform or joins a Center, in addition to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. By agreeing to it you confirm that you represent the Center or teacher and that you have the right to be bound by it.

1. Parties and roles

  • Mind (a project owned and run by an individual in the Arab Republic of Egypt) ("the Processor") provides the Platform.
  • The Center or teacher ("the Controller") decides what data is entered and for what purpose, and the Processor processes it on the Controller's behalf and on its instructions solely to operate the Platform.
  • A teacher working inside a Center is bound by this Agreement as it concerns them, and the Center is responsible for the compliance of its staff and teachers.

2. Representations and warranties of the Center

The Center represents and warrants, and this representation continues throughout use, that it:

  • holds every license, permit and registration required to carry on its educational or training activity in its country and governorate, that its activity is lawful, and that it will notify us immediately if any of them is revoked or suspended.
  • is registered commercially and for tax purposes where the law requires, and is solely responsible for the taxes, e-receipts and invoices due on its activity.
  • has a legal basis for every item of data it enters, and has notified data subjects that it is being entered into the Platform.
  • has obtained Guardians' consents where the law requires, and that a Guardian's consent through the Platform is an additional step that does not relieve it of its own obligation.
  • enters accurate and up-to-date data, especially the age group, and does not enter unnecessary data.
  • does not collect from children any additional data outside the Platform in breach of the law.
  • vets the people who work with it around children (teachers, assistants and reception staff) and is responsible for choosing, conduct and supervision of them.

3. Center responsibilities for staff and permissions

  • The Center is responsible for everything done through its staff accounts (teachers, assistants, reception, accountants) and gives them the least permissions necessary.
  • It withdraws the permissions of anyone whose relationship with it ends immediately.
  • It keeps Students' and Guardians' data confidential, does not use it beyond managing the activity, and does not share it with others except on a legal basis.
  • It is responsible for any wrongful or harmful use of data by its staff.

4. Attendance, payments and settlements

  • The Center is responsible for approving and correcting attendance and for verifying the accuracy of reports before relying on them.
  • The Center contracts directly with the payment provider, and fees reach its own account directly. It may not claim from the Processor any amount paid by a Student or Guardian or place on it any dispute over it.
  • The Center is responsible for recording cash amounts accurately and issuing their receipts, and for their matching what it actually received.
  • Percentages, rents, discounts and settlements between the Center, teachers and Guardians are calculated in the Platform according to settings that the Center itself defines. The Center is responsible for the accuracy of those settings and for any errors resulting from them, and the Platform does not guarantee their accuracy and is not a substitute for an agreement between the parties.
  • Tax documents issued by the Platform are an organizing tool, do not replace review by the Center's accountant, and do not guarantee compliance with tax law.
  • Refunds, cancellations and financial disputes are the Center's responsibility alone.

5. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor undertakes to:

  • process data to operate the service on the Center's instructions only.
  • apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures.
  • bind its personnel to confidentiality.
  • notify the Center of any breach affecting its data without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after becoming aware of it, so that the Center can meet its legal duty to report.
  • assist the Center to a reasonable extent in responding to data subjects' requests (access, correction and deletion).
  • delete or hand over the data when the contract ends within 30 days of the request, except what the law requires to be kept.
  • make available reasonable information demonstrating its compliance with this Agreement on request.

6. Sub-processors

The Center agrees that the Processor may use the hosting and notification providers named in the Privacy Policy, will be notified of any material change in them, and may object by terminating the Agreement.

7. Child protection

  • No child under 18 is registered, and their account is not activated, until their Guardian consents through the Platform.
  • The Center and its staff may not contact children outside the Center's approved framework using data obtained from the Platform.
  • The Center reports to the competent authorities, the Guardian and the Processor immediately on any incident of abuse of, or danger to, a child.
  • The Processor may suspend the account immediately on reasonable suspicion of abuse of a child, preserve evidence and cooperate with the competent authorities.

8. Subscription and fees

The subscription, its price and duration are set out on the subscription page at the time of subscribing, and the Terms of Use (Section 8) apply to it.

9. Indemnity

The Center shall indemnify the Processor, its officers, employees and providers and defend them against any claim, fine, penalty, loss, attorney's fees and court costs arising from: its educational or commercial activity, lack of a license or tax registration, data it entered without a basis, the absence of a Guardian's consent, the acts of its staff, or any dispute with a Student, Guardian, employee, teacher, government body or payment provider. This Section does not apply to what cannot lawfully be placed on the Center, or to what results from the Processor's fraud or gross negligence.

10. Limitation of the Processor's liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law: the Processor's total liability does not exceed the subscription fees the Center paid in the twelve months before the claim, and it is not liable for any indirect damages, loss of profit, or loss of data or reputation. This limit does not apply to what cannot lawfully be excluded, such as fraud and gross negligence.

11. Suspension and termination

  • The Processor may suspend the Center's account immediately if it is established, or reasonably suspected, that its license has lapsed, that it has broken the law, that it has abused a child, or that it has breached this Agreement, or if a competent authority so requires.
  • The Center may terminate the Agreement by deleting its workspace after exporting its data.
  • The Agreement applies throughout use of the Platform, and the indemnity, limitation of liability and confidentiality provisions remain in force after it ends.

12. Governing law and jurisdiction

The Agreement is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt and the courts of Cairo have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it, without prejudice to the mandatory rules of the Center's country. The parties will try to resolve the dispute amicably within 30 days of written notice before going to court.

13. Language

This Agreement is available in Arabic and English. The version in the language you selected when you accepted is the version you agreed to, and it prevails if there is any discrepancy between the language versions.
