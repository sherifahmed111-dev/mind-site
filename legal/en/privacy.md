# Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11-v3

Here we explain in detail what data we collect, why, who sees it, how long it stays, and your rights over it.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- Mind ("the Company") operates the Platform. It is owned and run by an individual in the Arab Republic of Egypt and is not yet a registered company.
- For the data of Students and Guardians that a Center enters or that arises from its activity (attendance, grades, payments), **the Center is the controller**, and the Company is a **processor** acting on the Center's instructions and under the data processing agreement.
- For the data of your own account and subscription, the Company is the controller.

## 2. Data we collect

We collect as little data as possible:

- **Account data:** name, email (for sign-in), and role (Center, teacher, assistant, reception, accountant, Guardian, Student).
- **Student data:** name, age group (under 16, 16 to 17, or 18 and over) instead of date of birth, and their membership in the Center, groups and subjects. We do not collect the Student's phone number or school grade.
- **Check-in and check-out:** time of recording, method and status (present, absent or late), and the result of verifying that the person is within the Center's area.
- **Location:** your device's location is compared with the Center's area only at the moment attendance is recorded. **We do not store your coordinates** and we do not track your location at any other time.
- **Academic activity:** grades, homework, the "what was covered today" summary, and completion certificates.
- **Payments:** amount, status, receipt number, who received the money, discounts, installments and balance. **We do not receive or store payment card data**; it stays with the payment provider.
- **Tax documents:** receipts, cancellation notices and invoices issued by the Platform with sequential numbers.
- **Consents:** a record of every consent (date, version, language, device and network address).
- **Technical data:** notification token, device type, app version, and network address at the time of consent.
- **Audit log:** who made a sensitive change and when (settings, permissions, discounts, transfers and freezes, certificate revocations).

## 3. Why we use your data

To operate the service only: recording attendance, schedules, grades, invoices and receipts, operational notifications, monthly and weekly reports, technical support, platform security and fraud prevention, and complying with the law and preserving evidence.

Basis for processing: your consent (or the Guardian's consent for a child), performing the service you asked for, and our legal obligations.

## 4. What we will never do

- We will not sell or rent your data.
- We will not use your personal data for advertising or targeted marketing, and we will not share it with advertisers. The Platform may show the Platform's own ads to parents and to Centers and staff, chosen only by the Center's country and governorate and not by your data, never shown to students, closable, and labelled "Ad". Centers on the Pro plan and subscribed parents see no ads.
- We will not build profiles of children's behavior or analyze their behavior for a commercial purpose.
- We will not share it with any party for a commercial purpose.
- We make no automated decisions that legally affect you. "Smart notes" (such as falling grades or low attendance) are simple statistics from the Center's own data, shown only to the teacher and the Guardian, and no automated decision is based on them.

## 5. Children

- An account for a Student under 18 is not activated, and their data is not collected, until their linked Guardian gives written (electronic) consent.
- A Guardian may view their child's data and withdraw consent at any time from Settings, which suspends the child's account.
- We apply the highest level of protection to everyone under 18.
- The Platform has no direct chat between a teacher and a child.

## 6. Who sees your data

According to role and permissions only:

- **Center and teacher:** see the data of their own Students and groups only. Assistants, reception and accountants see what their permissions allow within the Center.
- **Guardian:** sees the data of their own children linked to their account only.
- **Student (with an account):** sees their own data only.
- No user sees the data of another Center.
- Company staff do not look at Centers' data except when necessary to solve a technical problem, at the Center's request, or under a legal obligation.
- A certificate is verified only by someone who holds its code, and it shows only what is written on it.

## 7. Service providers

We use providers to operate the Platform, and we bind them by contract to protect the data and to use it only to serve us:

- Hosting, database and authentication: Supabase, and this project's servers are in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
- Notifications: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which receives the device token and the notification title and text (which may include the child's name).
- Online payment (for Centers that enable it later): a licensed payment provider contracted directly by the Center; card data does not pass through us. Cash or transfer payments are made directly between the parent and the Center, and the Platform only records them.
- The app stores (Apple and Google) for distributing the app and for subscriptions made through them.
- Password breach check: when you create a password, the app sends only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash to the Have I Been Pwned service to check whether it appears in known leaks. The password itself never leaves your device.

## 8. Where data is stored and transferred

Data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union), that is, outside Egypt, and the providers listed above may process some of it from other countries. By using the Platform, and on your consent, your data is transferred and stored outside Egypt, and we comply with Egyptian law conditions for that (such as a license, contractual safeguards and notifying the data subject).

## 9. How long we keep data

- We keep your data for as long as you use the Platform.
- Students with no activity for a period set by the Center have their names removed automatically, while financial and tax records remain without a name.
- Notifications are deleted after 90 days.
- Financial records and tax documents are kept for the period the law requires.
- **The consent record** remains even after the account is deleted because it is legal evidence.
- When an account is deleted, your personal data is erased except what the law requires us to keep.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data and download a copy (use "Download my data" in Settings), correct it, delete it, withdraw your consent, object to processing, and complain to the data protection authority in your country.

To delete your account: Settings → Delete account. For Student data entered by a Center, send your request to correct or delete it to the Center first, and we will help the Center carry it out.

We respond to your requests within the period set by law.

## 11. Security

We use encrypted connections, strict permissions at the level of each table and role, payment keys stored in an encrypted vault, and audit logs for sensitive operations. No system is 100% secure, and we do not guarantee that a breach will never occur.

If a breach affecting your data occurs, we notify the competent authority within the period the law sets (72 hours in Egypt and Saudi Arabia), and we notify those affected and the Center without undue delay.

## 12. Changes

We may amend this policy. For a material change we will ask for your consent again and show you the version number.

## 13. Contact

Data protection officer: the Platform owner (Mind). You may also file a complaint with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center.
